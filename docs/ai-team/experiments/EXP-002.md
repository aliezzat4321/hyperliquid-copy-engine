## Hypothesis
A causal, mechanical previous-day high/low liquidity-sweep reversal rule has positive execution-cost-adjusted expectancy on liquid crypto markets during the London morning, and any edge survives an untouched out-of-sample window.

## Frozen definition
This experiment was preregistered in issue #177 before the historical run. It is exploratory price-action research, not Hyperliquid execution validation.

- BTCUSDT and ETHUSDT Binance USD-M perpetuals; 5-minute OHLCV.
- Previous day = 00:00–23:59 UTC high/low.
- Trade window = 08:00–10:00 `Europe/London`, DST-aware.
- First eligible setup only per market/day.
- Short: bar trades above PDH and closes back below PDH; next 5-minute bar must close below the sweep-bar low.
- Long: bar trades below PDL and closes back above PDL; next 5-minute bar must close above the sweep-bar high.
- Entry = confirmation-bar close. Stop = sweep extreme plus a 2 bps buffer.
- Frozen target variants = 1.0R, 1.5R, 2.0R, 3.0R; otherwise forced exit before 10:00 London.
- If stop and target both touch inside one bar, the stop is scored first.
- 0.5% equity risk target per trade, capped at 5x notional/equity.
- 15 bps assumed round-trip execution cost on notional.
- Discovery = 2021-01-01 through 2024-12-31. Untouched holdout = 2025-01-01 through 2026-08-31.
- No parameter changes after observing the holdout.

## Data
Source: Binance public USD-M perpetual 5-minute kline archives, used only as a liquid-crypto proxy.

- BTCUSDT: 595,872 bars, 2021-01-01 00:00 UTC through 2026-08-31 23:55 UTC; no missing daily fallback archives.
- ETHUSDT: 595,872 bars over the same window; no missing daily fallback archives.
- 2,068 eligible days per asset had a complete 288-bar prior UTC day.
- BTC breached either PDH or PDL during 08:00–10:00 London on 695/2,068 days (33.61%); 190/2,068 days (9.19%) met the full sweep/reclaim/immediate-confirmation rule.
- ETH breached either level on 713/2,068 days (34.48%); 191/2,068 days (9.24%) met the full rule.

The social-media premise that the market sweeps a previous-day extreme every London morning is therefore not supported by this crypto proxy: even an intrawindow breach occurred on only about one-third of eligible days, and the frozen confirmed setup occurred on about 9.2%.

## Execution economics
The runner applies the frozen 15 bps round-trip cost to executed notional. Spread, market impact, latency and Hyperliquid L2 depth are not measured in this exploratory proxy. Funding is not modeled because all positions are intraday. A surviving candidate would still require Hyperliquid-specific L2/shadow validation before any promotion.

## Statistics
A deterministic 10,000-resample day-cluster bootstrap was used for a two-sided 90% confidence interval on mean net R. Four target variants were frozen ex ante and all are reported rather than selecting the best historical winner. The holdout sample passed the preregistered minimum-size thresholds (97 trades, 88 distinct days), and profit concentration was below the 50% exclusion threshold; however every target had negative net expectancy and a bootstrap interval entirely below zero.

## Result
`FAIL` — evidence level `EXPLORATORY`.

All four frozen holdout variants failed because net expectancy was non-positive, the 90% bootstrap lower bound was not above zero, and robustness across assets/years failed. No capital authorization follows from this experiment.

## Decision
Do not promote or trade this exact Instagram-style PDH/PDL London reversal rule. The experiment is useful as a negative result: the claimed daily occurrence is empirically false in the tested crypto proxy, and the frozen setup has no robust net edge.

A distinct future experiment may test a materially different, preregistered hypothesis (for example a different structural stop/confirmation or a BTC-specific regime) only as a new experiment. It must not retune EXP-002 after seeing this holdout. Any promising successor must ultimately use Hyperliquid-specific spread/depth/slippage and prospective shadow evidence before capital can be considered.

## Retest condition
Do not rerun this frozen rule merely with adjusted parameters. Retest only if there is new causal evidence supporting a materially different preregistered rule, or if Hyperliquid-specific execution data establishes that a separately defined successor hypothesis should be evaluated. Such work must receive a new experiment ID and an untouched validation window.
